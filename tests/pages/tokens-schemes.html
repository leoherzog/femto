Color schemes

Scheme tokens on every color-bearing element: on the page, in light and dark islands nested several deep both ways, on elements that carry data-theme themselves, in shadow roots whose host has no theme, a dark theme or a light theme, and under an author's inline color-scheme on an element without data-theme (a registered difference: light-dark() tokens follow it, 2.1.1's literals did not).
